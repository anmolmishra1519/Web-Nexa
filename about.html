Our Story

Started by One Person With One Goal

Web Nexa was founded by Anmol Mishra with a single mission — to make professional websites and software accessible to every startup and local business, regardless of budget. Too many small businesses were either priced out of the market or handed generic, low-quality work. Web Nexa exists to change that.

Every project at Web Nexa is built with the same care, speed, and quality — whether it's a local salon's website or a startup's custom software. Anmol personally leads every single project from the first conversation to the final delivery.

Based in Greater Noida, we work with clients across all of India. We communicate clearly, deliver fast, and make sure you love what we build — every time.
