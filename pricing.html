Common Questions About Pricing

Are these prices fixed?
QuickSite is a fixed price of ₹6,500. BizSite and Site Makeover are range-based because every business has different needs — your final quote is always confirmed and agreed upon before any work begins.
Are there any hidden charges?
None. What we quote is exactly what you pay. No registration fees, no maintenance fees, no surprise invoices.
What does the 1 month free support include?
Any bug fixes, minor content updates, or small changes within 30 days of delivery — at zero extra cost.
How do I pay?
We accept UPI, bank transfer, and Razorpay. A 50% advance is required to begin work, and the remaining 50% is paid upon delivery before the site goes live.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes. If you start with QuickSite and later want to expand to a multi-page BizSite, we can upgrade your existing project at any time.
What if I need something not listed here?
Just reach out. We handle custom requirements and will give you a tailored quote based on exactly what you need.
